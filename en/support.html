Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Only locally on your device (iOS: Files app → “On My iPhone” → Erbstück). There is no cloud and no account.

How do I back up my data?
Use “Share all data as ZIP” to create a complete backup at any time – e.g. email it to yourself or copy it to a USB stick. Your data is also included in your normal device backup.

How do I create the estate inventory?
Share button → “Create and share PDF”. If there are confidential entries, choose between the official inventory and your private complete version.

What does “confidential” mean?
Objects and accounts marked as confidential appear only in the private complete inventory. The official PDF omits them entirely – with no hint of gaps.

Can I dictate instead of typing?
Yes – via the microphone key on the iPhone keyboard. On modern iPhones dictation runs directly on the device.
